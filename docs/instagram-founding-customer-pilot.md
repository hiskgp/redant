# RedANT Instagram Automation — Founding Customer Pilot

## Offer

RedANT is inviting a small group of boutiques and clothing businesses to test Instagram enquiry automation. The pilot focuses on reducing repetitive replies and helping the team respond consistently to customer enquiries.

### Initial workflow under validation
- Detect eligible Instagram comment events for an enrolled professional account.
- When the configured keyword/trigger matches, send a permitted private reply using Meta's official APIs and within applicable platform rules.
- Track each automation run and let the business pause automation at any time.
- Add AI-powered DM answers only after the basic workflow, costs, and safety controls are validated.

Do not promise a feature until it has passed a live end-to-end test on an eligible account.

## Founding customer outreach (English)

Hi! We’re building RedANT, an Instagram enquiry automation tool for boutiques and small D2C brands. It aims to help you handle repetitive product questions and turn eligible post comments into useful private replies, so fewer enquiries get missed.

We’re speaking with a few businesses before finalising the pilot. Could I ask you 3 quick questions?
1. Roughly how many product enquiries do you receive through Instagram each day?
2. Which questions do you answer repeatedly (price, size, availability, delivery, etc.)?
3. If a reliable tool reduced this manual work, would you consider a paid pilot?

We’ll be transparent about the features currently available and won’t connect an account without your approval.

## Discovery tracker

For every conversation, record:
- Business name and public Instagram handle
- Approximate daily comments and DMs
- Top 3 repeated questions
- Current reply process and time spent
- Most valuable workflow
- Willingness to pay and objections
- Follow-up date and pilot decision

Collect only business-relevant details. Do not ask for Instagram passwords or access tokens.

## Pilot pricing hypothesis

Test willingness to pay rather than presenting these as final prices:
- One-time setup fee: quote after understanding the onboarding effort.
- Monthly subscription: test ₹499 / ₹999 / ₹1,999 tiers based on workflow and usage.
- Clearly disclose limits, support, AI usage charges, cancellation, and any third-party charges before accepting payment.
